The Eating Guide

A mix-and-match system for lowering cholesterol and weight without banning anything: build each day from pre-sized meals, batch-cook on Sundays, and let the swaps do the work. Everything on this site lives in four editable JSON files — change them and the site updates itself.

The daily calorie skeleton

The five levers, in order of impact

1. Cut alcohol to almost nothing

The liver turns alcohol — especially with sweet mixers — directly into triglycerides, and the calories are pure surplus. This is lever one for anyone with high triglycerides.

Triglycerides −20% or more is common

2. Trade refined carbs for intact ones

White rice, bagels, regular pasta, and baked goods spike blood sugar and the excess converts to triglycerides. Brown rice, whole-wheat pasta, oats, corn tortillas, and potatoes with skin do the same job with fiber attached.

Triglycerides down, steadier hunger

3. Swap saturated fat for unsaturated fat

Processed cheese, butter, fatty cuts, and cream drive LDL. Olive oil, nuts, avocado, and fish are the repair crew. It's a swap, not a cut — total fat stays moderate.

LDL −8 to −10%

4. Add soluble fiber every single day

Oats, beans, barley, apples, and citrus form a gel that drags cholesterol out through digestion. Oats most mornings, beans or lentils once a day.

LDL −5 to −10%

5. Eat fish twice a week, lose the weight

Fatty fish delivers omega-3s that lower triglycerides directly, and every 10 pounds lost is worth roughly 5–8 points of LDL and 15–20 of triglycerides.

Compounds every other lever

The weekly rhythm

  • Oats or another soluble-fiber breakfast at least 5 mornings
  • Beans, lentils, or edamame in at least one meal every day
  • Fish dinner twice a week
  • Red meat once a week, lean cut — rationed, not banned
  • A measured handful of nuts most days
  • Weigh in once a week, same day, same conditions
  • Sweets are scheduled (two real desserts a week), never grabbed

Build a day

Pick one breakfast, one lunch, one dinner, and two snacks — the tally bar tracks calories and protein. Your picks are remembered by this browser until you reset. Items marked ★ LDL-fighter carry the soluble fiber and omega-3s doing the heavy lifting. Click a meal's name (or the Recipe link) for the full ingredients and method — cards without one need no recipe. To add or edit meals, edit data/meals.json.

The Sunday batch cook

One two-hour session makes about 60% of the week automatic. Rotations alternate so week four tastes nothing like week one. Tap a step to check it off — checks are remembered by this browser. Edit rotations in data/batch.json.
